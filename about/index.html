About

I am Arman Arisman, an architect based in Indonesia. I’ve spent over 15 years in building and planning consulting, most of it at Penta Architecture, working in architectural computing—BIM, parametric modeling, generative design, and more recently AI—on real projects. I hold a doctoral degree from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), where my research grew directly out of that practice.

I write occasionally, usually after running into something on a project that a tool, a workflow, or a piece of software claimed to have already solved—and it hadn’t, not really. The gap between what these systems say a design decision is and what it actually takes to make that decision turns out to matter a lot in practice, not just on paper.

I’m also looking for ways back into teaching, since a lot of what I’ve learned the hard way on projects seems useful to students and younger practitioners figuring out their own footing.
