Computational Form-Finding · Tectonics · Spatial Phenomenology

Works

A collection of early-stage architectural form-finding and spatial studies developed across a wide range of computational design tools—from pure mathematical scripting and algorithmic code to parametric modeling and node-based workflows. Here, computation does not replace design intuition; instead, digital rules and systems generate raw geometric provocations—which are then curated, rationalized, and translated by the architect into tectonic structure, materiality, and lived spatial atmosphere.

01

Light-Sculpted Mass

Julia · Solar CSG

A generative CSG massing and solar ray-casting application in Julia calibrated for Bandung, coupling Boolean solid-void carving with daily direct sun-hour analysis.
02

Crystalline Monolith Pavilion

Python · Blender

A rule-based deconstructivist pavilion synthesized in Python using Blender's bmesh library, exploring interlocking opaque volumes and glazed voids.
03

Shard Museum

Julia · Procedural

A cultural museum composed of four tilted and twisted triangular prisms generated in Julia, balancing sharp deconstructivist geometry with warm vertical timber cladding over a reflecting pool.
04

Chaos Crystal

Julia · Computational

An urban cultural landmark synthesized via spherical radial perturbation and grid-based triangulation in Julia, translating mathematical mesh topology into folded zinc, concrete, and diagrid glass tectonics.
05

Eroded Monolith

Julia · Computational

A high-rise typology study using spherical voxel subtraction in Julia to carve a stepped vertical park and Corten-lined sky terraces into an orthogonal corner tower.
06

Flux Vaults

Julia · Parametric

A civic cultural concourse synthesized from parametric sine and cosine arch modulations in Julia, translating mathematical rib-lofting into interlocking concrete vaults and crescent clerestories.
07

Jenga Village

Julia · Voxel Logic

A modular tropical kampong housing study synthesized via stochastic block aggregation and discrete yaw rotation in Julia, translating shifted hexahedral volumes into interlocking courtyards and roof terraces.
08

Titanium Clash

Julia · Generative

An experimental deconstructivist massing study in Julia that collides eight tetrahedral shards into a cantilevered civic landmark clad in brushed titanium and inclined curtain walls.
